# Partner Brief

## What dbt-Workbench is

An MIT-licensed, self-hosted dbt control plane for lineage, catalog, run orchestration, scheduling,
SQL exploration, docs previews, workspace management, and optional RBAC.

## Who benefits

- Consulting teams delivering dbt implementations in private infrastructure
- Platform teams standardizing dbt operations for multiple analytics teams
- Regulated organizations with network or data-boundary restrictions
- Teams evaluating an open-source alternative to managed dbt workflows

## How a partner can help

- Validate deployment in a customer-like environment.
- Contribute adapter, security, or deployment improvements.
- Publish a technical walkthrough using the Docker demo.
- Refer an evaluation or provide implementation support.
- Share requirements and gaps through public GitHub issues.

## What to discuss before referring

- Supported warehouse and adapter requirements
- Authentication, RBAC, and network constraints
- Artifact retention and backup ownership
- Upgrade and rollback process
- Whether the customer needs commercial support or custom engineering
